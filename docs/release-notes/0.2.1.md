## Changed
- KernPage is now free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 (GPL-3.0). Anyone may use, share and change it, and anyone who shares a changed version must share its source code too. Version 0.2.0 and earlier stay available under MIT or Apache-2.0.
- KernPage's own text (menus, ribbon and dialogs) now uses the fonts that come with Windows, such as Segoe UI, instead of a font built into the program.
- The KernPage Launcher describes what KernPage does first: laying out pages, printing and creating PDFs. Opening Publisher files comes after.
- Contributors now sign a short contributor licence agreement (CLA.md) on their first pull request.

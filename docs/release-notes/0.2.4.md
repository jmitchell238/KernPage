## Added
- Print preview: the Print page shows each sheet of paper exactly as it will print, with arrows to step through the sheets ("Sheet 1, front", "Sheet 1, back").
- Print layouts: one page per sheet, several copies of a page on one sheet (business cards, labels, half-page flyers), several different pages on one sheet, and booklets. KernPage offers only the layouts that fit your page and paper.
- Booklets: print a folded booklet (for example a bulletin) on letter paper. KernPage puts the pages in the right order on each sheet, so you fold the printed sheets together and the pages read 1, 2, 3 and so on.
- Two-sided printing: print on both sides, flipping on the long or short edge, when your printer can do it by itself. Booklets choose this automatically. Printers that can't get a manual option: KernPage prints the fronts, tells you how to turn the stack over, then prints the backs.

## Changed
- Printing uses the paper size set on your printer, and a page smaller than the paper prints centred.

## Fixed
- Printing could stop with "The PDF renderer returned … pixels instead of …".
- KernPage no longer offers automatic two-sided printing on printers whose driver ignores it, and never prints a two-sided job one-sided without telling you.
